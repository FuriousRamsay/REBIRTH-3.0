# Camper and pickup lighting correction — 2026-10-23

Source and target: the installed cumulative REBIRTH 3.0 camper bundle and the current `RebirthUtils.dll` for 7 Days to Die 3.0 b259. The owner's daytime/nighttime screenshots show a broad sideward forward beam, bright intermittent white pickup rear illumination, and red and white pickup lamps that do not activate together.

## Confirmed code behavior

`RebirthCamperRearLights.Postfix` overrides native `VPHeadlight.Update` for `rebirthCamper80s`. Its former forward spot was 48 degrees with a 3-degree downward rotation. The pickup's red segments used `Calculate` with velocity forced to zero, while white segments were reverse-only. The white beam used a 5x multiplier, a 6 m range, and a 60-degree angle. That creates separate activation rules and makes the white beam brighter than red.

The installed bundle has separate pickup lamp lenses and bezels. Exact installed mesh inspection identified two `Rear lamp bezel` meshes and six `Rear lamp lens segment` meshes for pickup-only bodywork. The camper body module has a shared-light mesh containing its rear housing geometry; its rear-lens geometry is a separate renderer. The older Unity authoring prefab is not the current installed model and must not replace it.

## Implemented

Both pickup red and white segments now derive from the same brake/reverse/headlight state. White emission is 25% of red; the white projected beam is a further 12% of that emission, with a 1.5 m range and 36-degree angle. Red remains the dominant rear lamp. The camper rear lamps retain their red beam on reverse. The camper forward spots are 34 degrees and aimed 7 degrees downward from the model forward axis to focus road illumination ahead.

The first casing attempt did not alter rendered vertices. The mesh's LOD0 vertices live in an embedded `.resS` stream; that attempt changed only an inline copy and bounds. A decoded-mesh comparison found zero actual vertex changes despite the earlier report of 128. This was corrected by editing the streamed vertices for LOD0 and inline vertices for LOD1/2. The current candidate enlarges all four complete housings by 20%, moves their metal pieces 9 mm rearward, and enlarges the four red lenses by 10% across all three detail levels. Reopening the saved bundle confirms 324/600/294 housing vertices and 192/552/276 lens vertices changed in LOD0/1/2 respectively. The candidate was installed with the game closed. The separate solid red disc on each side has not yet been identified or changed.
